The Best of Dao

An exploration of the enduring wisdom of Laozi and Zhuangzi: how to live with simplicity, flexibility and awareness, without forcing life against its natural course.

The Dao does not ask us to withdraw from life. It asks us to see more clearly how life already moves.

What is Daoism?

Daoism, also written Taoism, is one of the great philosophical traditions of China. Its best-known classical texts are the Dao De Jing, associated with Laozi, and the Zhuangzi.

Daoist thought examines the relationship between human action, nature, power, freedom and the limits of language. It teaches that wisdom does not always come from exerting greater control. Sometimes it comes from understanding change, reducing unnecessary effort and acting in harmony with circumstances.
